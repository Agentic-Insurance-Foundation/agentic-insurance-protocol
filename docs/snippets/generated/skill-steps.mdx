<Steps>
<Step title={"Understand the need"}>

Ask what the customer wants to insure, where they live (the country, and the state in the United States), where the insured good is, and what they want the cover to do, in their own words. Record this statement and the time. Tell the customer that you do not hold an insurance licence, and that a licensed seller sells the product. As soon as you know the country, call `get_country_rules` on the registry with the country, the subdivision and the line, and follow every file of the result as well as this skill. When a file is stricter than this skill, follow the file. Keep the `version` of each file for the evidence. When the registry does not answer, read the bundled files in `references/countries/<code>/`: `country.md`, then the file of the state in `states/` and the file of the line in `lines/` when they exist, and tell the customer which version you used. When no file exists for the country, tell the customer that AIP has no rules for that country yet.

</Step>
<Step title={"Find a licensed seller"}>

Call `search_distributors` with the country, the subdivision, the line and the insured family, and `min_conformance_level: 3` when the customer wants to apply through you, or 2 to compare prices. Each result carries a `licence_check` for this market. When it is at most 7 days old, it is your check of that seller: act on its verdict as the table below says, and keep it. The order of the results is random, so never show it as a ranking. When the customer names a seller, or you find one anywhere else, call `get_distributor` on its server. A seller whose server your host connected counts as one that you found elsewhere. The address of the server is the only proof of its domain. You know it when you called the `mcp_url` yourself, or when your host gives you the address of each server that it connected. When you do not know it, you cannot check the seller: treat its licence as `unknown`, tell the customer why, and send it no answers. When you know it, the `domain` of `get_distributor` must be the domain of that address. When it is not, stop with that server. When that domain is the `domain` of a listing in your search, use that listing and its `licence_check`. Otherwise call `verify_licence` with `capacity: "distributor"`, that domain, the `identifier` of its licence in the customer's country and its `legal_name` as `get_distributor` gives them, the country, the subdivision (required in the United States) and the line. Do all this before you send any answer of the customer.

</Step>
<Step title={"Keep the products that fit"}>

Call `list_products` with the country, the subdivision, the line, the insured family and the kind, for example `cat`. The server returns only the products whose structured scope fits the customer. Read the `conditions` of each product against what the customer said, and ask the customer when you are not sure. Drop a product when the customer's own words show that a condition excludes them. When the insurer in [`Product.provider`](/0.1/reference#product) is not the seller, call `verify_licence` with `capacity: "provider"` and one of its `licences`, or its domain, and act on the verdict as for the seller. When no product fits, tell the customer and send no answers. Show the tiers of each product that fits side by side, with what each one covers and no price, because the price comes from `quote`. Each product gives its documents in `documents`. When the customer asks for them before a price, give them from there.

</Step>
<Step title={"Ask the questions and quote"}>

Call `quote` with the `product_id` and the answers that the customer already gave, each with its source. You can send no answers at all. On `needs_input`, the result gives the questions in `answers_schema`, and the `missing_fields`. Ask each missing question in plain words, from its `description` when it has one. Give a source for each answer, as the table of sources below says. Never ask for an identity number, a bank account or card number, a tax number, health data of a person or a password: the customer gives them on the seller's page. Call `quote` again with all the answers. Show the offers side by side, from their own fields, with no ranking: the premium, its period and whether taxes are in it, the fees, the cover, the limits, the excess and its basis, the main exclusions, the waiting period of each cover, the insurer and `valid_until`. Say that the price can change after the insurer's checks, and that the seller's page shows the firm price before the customer approves. Follow the country file on comparison and recommendation. On `no_offer`, give the customer its `message`. To compare sellers, quote with each one, and apply only with the seller that the customer chooses.

</Step>
<Step title={"Apply"}>

When the customer chooses an offer, call `submit_application` with its `offer_id` and the parties, when the seller declares level 3. A seller at level 2 takes no application: give the customer the `handoff_url` of the offer instead. Do not send the answers again: the seller takes the answers and their sources from the offer. Check that the `summary` of the result gives the price of the offer, and each answer with its source.

</Step>
<Step title={"Get the customer's approval"}>

Get the approval of the person who will be the subscriber. Give them the `url` of the result, and keep the `application_key` to yourself. When your host opens the page through MCP URL-mode elicitation, let it. On the seller's page, they read the documents, check each answer and its source, see the firm price, and approve. Then they sign and pay on the same site, and the seller sends them the contract. Their approval confirms that the answers are true and complete, so do not ask again. When they find a wrong answer, they refuse on the page. Correct the answer and its source, call `quote` again, and apply for an offer of the new quote. When they say they approved, call `get_operation` with the `application_id` and the `application_key`. The state `approved` means only that they approved. You are not told whether they paid, so do not say that the contract is concluded. Then give the customer the copy of the evidence that `references/evidence.md` describes, with the seller's `complaints_url`.

</Step>
